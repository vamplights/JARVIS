# Jarvis beta 0.1.0 — signal-integrity audit


## Changelog — beta 0.1.0, Part 2 (2026-10-13)

The previous research and integrity implementation is retained. This update changes the signal policy and starts new settings cohorts; it does not optimize parameters against historical holdouts. The version is beta 0.1.0 in the package, browser, sidebar and local health endpoint. There are no order routes or real trading interfaces.

### A. Strategy policy and costs

- One-minute source candles remain collected for opening-range construction, while evaluation refuses to emit new 1m signals. Defaults become stock 3m/5m/15m and crypto 1h/daily, with optional 5m/15m crypto research. Plain momentum is off and momentum-retest is restricted to 3m/5m. An explicit beta migration preserves journals and fee preferences, backs up the saved settings and begins a new cohort. Saved frozen theses retain their original levels and cannot enroll in the new cohort.
- The stop floor is the largest of the swing distance, configured ATR floor (0.75 default), market floor (25 bps stocks / 40 bps crypto), and four round-trip costs. Round-trip basis points are 2 × fee + 2 × slippage + spread; a fresh live spread takes precedence. Actual dollar fees/slippage also account for entry and exit notionals. A widened floor recomputes target, size and net target R and does not fail the old maximum-stop check solely because it was widened. Every signal displays costs as a percentage of original price risk. Crypto venue/fee settings and a default-fee warning make the venue assumption visible.
- Opening eligibility uses the first five-minute volume against 14 actual prior first-five-minute volumes, the requested RVOL/gap/news/earnings conditions, and price/volume/daily-ATR minimums. Missing opening history is not zero volume. Scan-universe history is warmed locally; watchlist plus eligible in-play stocks receive intraday evaluation. Opening lists are archived by date and settings cohort. A late opening list is shown in coverage but cannot masquerade as an on-time ORB decision.
- Added individually toggleable ORB-in-play, SPY/QQQ late-day momentum, post-earnings drift and daily trend following. ORB-in-play uses the top 20 opening RVOL ranks, first-candle direction, strict stop-entry trade-through, a daily-ATR stop subject to the cost floor, and 15:59 ET exit. SPY/QQQ momentum measures previous close to the complete 10:00 ET candle set, signals at 15:30 and exits at 15:59. PEAD uses timestamped EPS surprise when known or the actual reaction-session gap/RVOL; it waits for the next session and counts a maximum of 20 trading sessions. Daily trend uses 50/200-day SMAs, positive 63/126/252-session returns, a 3-ATR trail and the 50-day-close exit. Swing results remain separate from intraday results.
- Fixed, ATR-trailing and swing-trailing paper exits share the same issued signals and original risk reference. Trailing levels apply only after their observation bar; the original thesis remains frozen. Market entries use subsequent observations. Limits and stop entries require strict trade-through, record missed fills, and use stop-first handling when a bar is ambiguous. Entries beyond the frozen target or regular entry tolerance are missed fills. Daily plans can wait overnight for next-session data; intraday no-entry windows still apply to intraday signals.
- Daily stock candle completion, chart data and replay timing use actual ET session closes, DST and early closes. Crypto day boundaries stay at 00:00 UTC. ADX and efficiency are measured on closed signal-timeframe bars when history suffices; insufficient values remain null, not an invented zero. Neither indicator adds a new hard gate.

### B. News and movers

- Added a local News + Movers tab, public S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 membership caches, sector-fund comparisons to SPY, gainers/losers, volume/gap/52-week flags, and a ranked maximum of 15 research ideas. Basic mover screening is separate from full strategy evaluation. Generic market stories do not become issuer-specific catalysts for a quiet stock.
- Public Yahoo feeds, permitted user-configured IR/RSS feeds, and existing SEC metadata feeds retain their source and timestamp. SEC is skipped until a descriptive User-Agent with a contact email is configured. Requests are serialized/cached, same-host spacing is at least 300 ms, rate limits cool down, and unavailable sources are skipped without login or paywall workarounds. Bloomberg and paid services are not used.
- Headline themes use transparent local rules for rate cuts, oil, chip restrictions, tariffs, defense and AI investment. Help/hurt mappings are labeled possible impact, with the actual headline and risks. These are associations, not verified causes or a paid language-model service.
- Daily idea lists freeze with their actual publication time and settings cohort. Forward 1-week/1-month/3-month measurements start from the next session open and compare matching SPY dates. Track-record days and sample sizes are explicit. Outages do not create retrospective news recommendations.

### C. Auto paper outcomes

- Added a comprehensive local auto-paper ledger and tab, separate from the manual journal and old ledger. Calibrated setups at or above the user cutoff (40% default) are observed; uncalibrated setups require confirmation and retain that label. Every qualifying signal uses fixed dollar risk (15 default), without shared cash, trade-cap or loss-lockout exclusions.
- A separate Realistic account scenario applies saved-cohort capital, risk percentage/dollar limits, whole stock shares, entry costs, cash reservations, daily caps and sticky consecutive-loss lockouts in actual fill/arrival order. Those rules are applied globally before filtering by market or intraday/swing view. Alternative exit scenarios do not consume each other's cash.
- Records freeze probability, expected R, model version, settings, original stop and costs. Probability buckets, positive/nonpositive expected R, calibrated/uncalibrated, paired exit styles and settings cohorts remain distinct. Model training/graduation use the primary configured exit style and eligible confirmed outcomes; raw calibrated research outcomes remain tagged raw.
- The UI charts cumulative realized P&L with win/loss trade markers, daily realized P&L and cumulative results, per-trade prices/R/dollars/exit reasons, summary statistics and separate open liquidation values. Crypto days use UTC. Local per-day JSON reports store those chart series and are linked from report cards. Older same-day reports are archived per cohort instead of overwritten by a different strategy configuration.
- Limits: these are underlying stock/spot simulations, not option trades. They do not model borrow availability/fees, queue priority, market depth or a complete intrabar path. Prices are observed references and P&L deducts configured fees, slippage and spread. Correlated/overlapping trades can make ordinary trade-bootstrap confidence intervals too narrow. A small sample does not establish an edge.

### D. Local operation and recovery

- Daily cycles, scanner queues, pollers and signal callbacks isolate their errors and retry on later ticks, with a local error log. Shared history locks prevent quote polling or shorter concurrent downloads from overwriting a longer requested archive. Dataset/track-record caching avoids repeatedly parsing the same long histories on every browser poll.
- Startup reads the last heartbeat, backfills available stock/crypto history, and marks missed opens/cycles and backfilled report cards. Recovery handles outages beginning after noon and crypto UTC days across weekends. It resolves actually recorded signals only; missing final prices remain unmeasurable. It never invents historical model forecasts or frozen idea lists.
- `data/local-health.json` updates every minute. `/health` and `/api/health` identify the local app, version, heartbeat, uptime, stage and last error. The health file allows the local supervisor to detect a hung event loop.
- Included one-time NSSM setup, a heartbeat supervisor and post-reboot verification script plus README. The installer configures auto-start/restart and requested no-sleep timeouts under the user's Windows account so existing DPAPI authorization remains readable. Familiar Start/Restart scripts recognize the service. Service installation needs the user's Windows credentials and administrative execution; installation and reboot survival are not claimed without running those steps. No remote JARVIS server, paid API, cloud model or order endpoint is required.

### E. Review pipeline and validation

- The local daily sequence is history refresh → scanner → paper resolution → fixed-parameter replay/model training and held-out validation → report card plus saved paper chart series. Separate daily/hour history replay covers the new holding horizons. Primary-exit forward paper outcomes feed the existing separate stock/crypto profit models; the earlier closed-candle feature checks, chronological embargo and baseline/Brier validation remain in force.
- Ordinary and special strategy validation uses fixed parameters with disjoint chronological entry windows and a 12-bar purge. Old/new settings profiles never pool. ORB-in-play historical runs require an actually archived cohort-specific opening list; PEAD requires timestamped earnings metadata. Missing prerequisites yield no fabricated samples. The default public fallback exclusion for model fitting is retained.
- Added tests for migration/timeframes, costs/floors, opening eligibility and new strategies, reference fills, stop/limit trade-through, expiry, paired trails, account caps/cash/order, profile/report separation, model input causality, movers/themes/headlines, public request throttling, history concurrency/completion, service script configuration, local API restrictions and outage recovery. Full test result and live endpoint checks are recorded in the beta verification deliverable after installation.

## Changelog — confluence, provider provenance and validated profit estimates (2026-10-13)

This implements the attached 13-part update. No orders, brokerage account positions, automatic strategy tuning or out-of-sample parameter selection were added. The removed paper-account controls remain removed. “Paper outcomes” below are passive forward observations, with later fresh quotes and explicitly unmeasurable missing exits. Existing journal entries, old cohorts and source archives are preserved.

1. **One combined plan.** Strategies on the same symbol, market, timeframe, candle close and source produce one card, one size and one confirmed observation. The complete strategy list and confluence count are frozen as model inputs. A deterministic conservative primary plan supplies exact levels; stops/targets are never averaged. Opposite directions on that candle become **Conflicted – no trade**, size zero, with no alert or training label. Active opposing plans on the same symbol also block each other across timeframes; stale/expired plans do not cancel a current idea. Different candles/timeframes retain separate frozen level records. Historical single-timeframe replays cannot reproduce the live cross-timeframe conflict gate and are labeled candle-rule research, not a combined live portfolio. Original setup-family keys still prevent recording a direct/retest variation twice. Cohort-tagged IDs prevent a settings change from borrowing an earlier plan's prediction.
2. **Plain English.** Detail panels explain EMA, VWAP, breakouts and retests in ordinary language while preserving the entry, stop, target and R values. Unconfirmed patterns are described as still waiting. Multiple strategies appear together. Numeric heuristic evidence confidence is removed from signal cards, option plans and the main research view; internal checklist gates remain intact. The optional leverage table retains its rule band, separately from profit probability.
3. **Provider fallback and provenance.** Stocks prefer available fresh Robinhood data, then Yahoo Finance; a working CNBC public endpoint is a secondary stock fallback. Existing Coinbase public crypto data remains primary, with Yahoo as its fallback. Every new bar and signal carries source/fallback tags. Mixed-provider indicator input is rejected; changing provider replaces the active indicator series and marks an older provider's plan stale. Forward exits cannot silently switch provider. Fallback badges are visible. Public chart prices are not invented bid/ask quotes, so the existing verified-spread gate can keep these setups on Watch. Unofficial sources can fail or be delayed. Fallback samples are excluded from model training and graduation by default; the explicit setting changes this inclusion, not the recorded history. Unlabeled legacy archives used in new batched replay are treated as unverified for model inclusion.
4. **Downloaded history.** `public-market.mjs`, `/api/history`, the Refresh history button and `scripts/refresh-history.mjs` store datasets separately under symbol/interval/provider. Stock downloads request up to 28 days of 1m data, 59 days of 5m/15m data, and six months of daily data by default; crypto requests 7 days of 1m, 28 days of 5m, and six months of 15m/daily data. Provider availability is authoritative, not promised coverage. The 59-day Yahoo window avoids its observed 60-day boundary failure. Coinbase pages use at most 299 intervals, below its documented 300-candle limit. Downloads merge incrementally within one provider, retain real timestamps, record gaps/partial failures, and never fill holes with synthetic candles. Cached fallback data restores visibly stale after restart. See [Coinbase's primary candle reference](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles).
5. **Per-strategy controls.** Settings expose EMA period/slope lookback, retest search window/touch ATR, consolidation length/maximum ATR width, breakout body, volume average/RVOL, higher-frame on/off/shared inheritance and per-market timeframe, swing-stop buffer, ATR/bps stop minima, and target R. Stocks and crypto have independent strategy/frame enable lists within supported evaluation frames. All affect the actual replay/live rules. Parameter, cost and risk changes create a new settings cohort; older results remain stored and are not pooled into the new model. Alert preferences and report schedules do not reset performance cohorts.
6. **Separate profit models and expected R.** A deterministic L2-regularized logistic model fits stocks and crypto separately. Inputs include price-pattern features available at candle close, direction/timeframe, ET hour, confluence count and strategy membership. Training samples use unique non-overlapping confirmed, uncapped replay/forward outcomes from the same settings cohort. Future feature timestamps are rejected. Probabilities are withheld until the strategy/timeframe/market cohort has **200 earlier training outcomes over 15 sessions**, plus sufficient later validation. Predictions shrink toward that cohort's training base rate with weight `n/(n+200)`. Feature contributions show probability-point effects relative to training averages. Expected R and its deterministic 90% bootstrap interval are estimated directly from actual same-cohort realized R, including time exits, not inferred from a win rate or assumed 2R wins. Probability and expected R concern the underlying setup; they do not estimate option profitability. Predictions and their model version freeze when observed.
7. **Chronological validation.** Later sessions are held out; training outcomes must close at least 12 chart bars before the validation boundary. Standardization, class base rate, coefficients and expected R use training data only. No random train/test split or model/strategy hyperparameter search is used. Reported Brier, base-rate Brier, Brier skill `1-model/baseline`, and reliability bins evaluate held-out predictions. Skill at or below zero is labeled **No edge over baseline** and blocks model alerts. A small or absent sample has no manufactured score.
8. **Strategy graduation.** Each strategy × timeframe × market is Collecting, Candidate, Graduated or Suspended. Candidate requires at least 100 forward confirmed outcomes with positive mean R. Graduation requires the next 100 genuinely fresh entries after the Candidate boundary to qualify, and the full-sample 95% bootstrap lower bound above zero. Graduation persists until an explicit suspension condition: recent 100-trade CI upper bound below zero, or strategy drawdown above `maxStrategyDrawdownR` (15R). A negative short-window average alone does not suspend. All active cohorts, including empty ones, are counted. Shared confluence outcomes can inform each member strategy, so multiple-testing/correlation warnings remain visible; roughly 400–600 trades may be needed for a real ~0.1R edge with ordinary trading variance. Trade bootstrap intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted and may be too narrow for correlated observations.
9. **Browser alerts.** New eligibility can produce a configurable tone, card highlight, tab-title pulse and optional desktop notification. Defaults require Graduated (Candidate opt-in), expected R ≥0.15, profit probability ≥0.75, positive held-out model skill, a confirmed fresh non-fallback/non-conflicted setup, and acceptable option spread risk when present. Settings include on/off, tone, volume, probability/EV thresholds, local quiet hours, symbol cooldown and alerts/hour. Startup history/repeated polls are suppressed; the server deduplicates and records accepted alerts. Browser sound needs a user click; desktop notification permission is requested only from the explicit test/enable action. Reduced-motion preference uses a static highlight. Alert notices offer Taken/Skipped journal actions.
10. **No-trade events.** The existing `market-context.mjs` event filter is extended, not duplicated. CPI/FOMC support full-day or ±N-minute blocks; FOMC window mode uses the explicitly disclosed 14:00 ET assumption when the official calendar supplies only a date. Earnings blackout days and the first-five-minutes stock block are configurable. Reasons appear on cards. Historical replay retains its existing exclusion of unavailable point-in-time earnings/macro/VIX context; it does enforce deterministic opening/no-entry windows. An independent saved no-entry window can still block a time when the event-specific toggle is off.
11. **Options reality check.** A qualified fresh contract can show delta/theta/spread-based estimated P&L at the underlying target and stop over the 12-bar horizon. It is labeled an estimate ignoring IV changes, gamma and fill uncertainty. Round-trip spread above 25% of the plan's risk (configurable) blocks option eligibility/alerts. Separate option observations enter at a subsequent fresh ask and exit at a fresh bid near the underlying exit or timeout, with explicit per-contract cost assumptions. Missing Greeks/quotes or an entry arriving after the underlying exit cannot create a fabricated option outcome. Underlying R is never replaced by estimated option returns.
12. **Personal journal comparisons.** Taken/skipped records include a reason dropdown and notes, actual fill/exit, size and, for options, verified multiplier. Comparison reports show adverse fill slippage, actual versus observed-system R, skipped-winner and early-exit R/cash scenarios, and rule breaks for unalerted or over-budget trades. Cash opportunity figures use the frozen reference size (one observed contract for option examples) and are explicitly hypothetical. Options compare to separately observed option results/full premium risk, not underlying price-stop R. Manual net P&L remains user-entered; no account execution is introduced.
13. **Daily cycle and saved review.** While running, JARVIS schedules history refresh → fixed-rule replays → passive outcome resolution → model fit/held-out validation → report card after the configured ET hour (17:00 default). Reports show strategy/graduation counts, after-cost average R/win rate, yesterday's predictions/reliability/Brier trend, three losing patterns, source/fallback usage and journal rule breaks. Past report files can be opened by date. Insufficient samples, missing exits and partial provider failures stay explicit. A causal replay cache and batched strategy workers reduce repeated indicator work; regression tests compare cached/batched results exactly with uncached/single-run calculations, including gaps and appended future data.

**Verification:** `node --test` passes 150 tests on the compatible Node 24 runtime, including Windows account encryption. New regression coverage includes confluence/freeze/deduplication, conflicts, settings, source isolation/switches, download limits/gaps, future-feature rejection, chronological fitting, baseline failure, expected-R intervals, graduation/suspension, alert eligibility/throttling, options observations, journal comparisons, daily persistence/scheduling, API protections and replay cache equivalence. Browser checks covered real fallback candles, combined detail panels, settings saving, the history table, model guardrails and report-card rendering. Real initial download: **100 nonempty datasets / 351,420 bars**, with zero partial-download failures after retrying the provider boundary; genuine missing intervals remain recorded. Initial mock review completed 424 fixed-strategy runs. These selected-symbol data and recent outcomes do **not** establish an edge; no parameters were changed in response to replay results.

## Changelog — cost-aware R measurement (2026-10-13)

This update measures after-cost expectancy and uncertainty. It does not establish a profitable edge, optimize parameters, or select settings from out-of-sample results. Existing confirmed levels remain frozen; closed-candle evaluation, next-bar historical fills, stop-first ambiguity, worse-open stop gaps, 12-bar embargoes, local request protections, and absence of order routes are retained.

1. **Paper-trading option removed.** The enable/disable form and `/api/paper` / `/api/paper/settings` routes are removed. The Signal Outcomes page uses passive forward observations. On loading an old ledger, new account-position simulation is disabled. Historical records and the maintenance of already-open legacy positions are preserved; they are not retroactively included in the new R sample. `paper-ledger.mjs` and its filename remain for data compatibility.
2. **Market-specific costs.** Stock defaults are 0.3 bps fees per side, 2 bps slippage per side and 2 bps round-trip spread; crypto defaults are 60/3/2. Legacy flat costs migrate to crypto only. A one-time `journal-settings.json.before-edge-migration` backup preserves the original journal/settings. Sizing, evaluation, replay, legacy ledger accounting, learning forecasts and derivatives planning use the appropriate market. Usable fresh bid/ask replaces an assumed spread in live signal measurement. Historical replays use explicitly assumed spreads because historical bid/ask is unavailable.
3. **R definition clarified.** `netRR` now means net target return in units of original price-stop risk, consistent with realized R. `netRewardRisk` separately reports the conventional net gain / (price loss + costs) ratio. With a $400 entry, $399 stop and $402 target, stock defaults leave about 1.736R at the target; the conventional ratio is about 1.373. It is mathematically impossible for that latter ratio to exceed 1.5 under these assumptions. This distinction is displayed and tested rather than concealing costs in a changed denominator. Position sizing still budgets the loss plus costs.
4. **Stop noise floors.** A stop must be at least 0.75 ATR and 15 bps for stocks / 25 bps for crypto. A closer five-bar swing stop is widened, then size, target, structural cap and cost-adjusted R are recalculated. Signals record `stopATR` and `stopBps`; confirmed stops never tighten or repaint after confirmation.
5. **Passive forward outcomes.** Observations freeze their setup and settings profile. A subsequent fresh quote is required for an entry reference, avoiding backdated fills. Stops/targets resolve conservatively from later quotes or fully subsequent closed bars. Expiry uses a fresh quote around the deadline or the final closed bar; missing closing data produces an unmeasurable outcome, not fabricated P/L. Rows record realized R after fees/slippage/spread, gross R, cost R, favorable/adverse excursions, ADX, efficiency, cohort, regime and Eastern observation hour. Confirmed and raw cohorts are separate; targets below `minRR` are flagged as capped.
6. **Statistics.** `/api/stats`, `scripts/analyze-ledger.mjs` and the Signal Outcomes table report n, win rate, average after-cost R, profit factor and deterministic 95% bootstrap intervals, grouped by market, strategy, timeframe, direction, regime, Eastern hour, cohort, target cap and settings profile. Headlines use confirmed, uncapped observations only. Existing records without measured R are excluded. n < 100 is explicitly insufficient. Trade-bootstrap intervals assume independent trades; overlap and shared market moves can make them too narrow. More observations alone do not establish an edge.
7. **Strategy/timeframe defaults.** New installations use stocks 3m/5m, no 1m strategy defaults, and disable plain momentum via an empty frame list. Each variation has its own `strategyFrames` list. Existing saved preferences remain in force. To explicitly adopt the new stock-frame and strategy-frame defaults once, launch `node server.mjs --migrate-strategy-frames`, or set `JARVIS_MIGRATE_STRATEGY_FRAMES=1` for that launch, then clear the environment flag. Cost/schema migration is automatic and independent. The Settings form supports 5m stocks and manual per-strategy frame editing; no auto-optimization is performed.
8. **Eastern entry windows.** Defaults block stock entries at 09:30–09:35 and 11:30–13:30 ET. Start is inclusive and end exclusive; DST conversion uses the time-zone database. The live gate and historical entry checks both apply these windows. They do not close existing positions or block crypto.
9. **Useful archived-session learning.** Automatic replay loads up to the newest 20 complete prior sessions from `data/sessions`, even when the associated live connection is unavailable. Current and incomplete sessions are excluded. Fixed-parameter walk-forward runs retain unique out-of-sample trades. Latest runs are pooled across symbols by market/strategy/timeframe and settings version using trade R, never summed dollar P/L. Pooling excludes development trades, duplicates and capped targets, and displays expectancy with confidence intervals. Old zero-trade single-split runs remain historical records but do not enter the new pool.
10. **Regime measurements.** Wilder ADX(14) and the existing efficiency ratio are recorded on each signal. ADX is not an entry gate and no threshold was selected using replay results.

Validation includes known cost/ADX arithmetic, live-spread substitution, floor sizing, migration backups and opt-in flags, frozen theses, no-entry boundaries/DST, causal outcomes, raw/capped exclusion, bootstrap reproducibility, archived-session completeness, OOS deduplication, server routes, UI controls and the existing regression suite. Software tests do not validate trading profitability.

Scope: the regular Node/browser application, its signal engine, replay, session learning, market-data adapters, paper ledger, and UI. This is a code and regression-test audit, not certification that a strategy is profitable or that every possible defect has been found.

## Findings and corrections

| Finding | Correction / practical effect |
|---|---|
| Re-evaluating an already confirmed setup could replace its entry, stop and score. | Freeze the confirmed closed-candle thesis. Current quotes, context and entry gates can change eligibility, but not issued levels. |
| Expiration logic could overwrite terminal invalidation. | An invalidated ID remains invalidated after its entry window expires. |
| Aggregation relied on input order and accepted unsupported source/timeframe ratios. | Sort input and require whole multiples; incomplete or missing component candles cannot confirm a setup. |
| Higher-timeframe EMA could span an intraday data hole. | Reset warmup after gaps; permit ordinary overnight equity gaps. |
| Opening-range inputs could include synthetic/incomplete bars or unsorted raw bars. | Require every opening minute to be complete, ordered and present. |
| Confirmed thesis storage existed only in memory. | Persist frozen engine snapshots and restore them under identical settings across restarts. |
| VWAP could bridge volume gaps or miss its regular-session anchor after premarket input. | Require contiguous session bars and restart at the actual anchor; provisional candles cannot contribute. |
| Flat EMA slopes were labeled bearish. | Use a neutral zero direction for flat slopes. |
| Live WebSocket sequence tracking was shared across channels. | Track sequence numbers per channel to avoid false recovery/replay decisions. |
| Historical entry R:R was tested at the signal price instead of the next-bar fill. | Recheck direction, tolerance and net R:R at the modeled fill. Costs remain explicit. |
| Adjacent development and test outcomes could overlap. | Separate chronological windows with a 12-bar embargo matching the outcome horizon. Test periods do not overlap. |
| A long walk-forward run could silently stop after 20 folds and omit its tail. | Default window width covers available history in at most 20 folds, includes a final partial window, and rejects an excessive explicit fold count. |
| Revised source bars could overwrite the forward-learning record. | Retain first-observed closed-bar values in the learning archive. Revised source data remain a limitation of independent historical imports. |
| Old and revised confidence methods could share calibration cohorts. | Include the Beta 0.05 protocol in the settings/profile fingerprint. Older observations are retained but not silently pooled into the new cohort. |
| A raw detection could bypass the latest risk-gated status during paper entry. | Paper entry consults the latest eligible gated signal and requires it to remain confirmed. |
| Paper candle-exit cursor could start undefined. | Initialize it at actual simulated entry. Candles containing pre-entry prices cannot resolve the trade. |
| A throttle could discard newly drained signal observations. | Use persistent IDs for deduplication; do not drop newly detected signals due to polling frequency. |
| Paper option exits could lose coverage when the selected chain changed. | Continue polling held contract IDs, rotating batches of at most 20. |
| A verified zero option bid was treated as missing. | Preserve zero bids for conservative liquidation marks; new candidates still require a positive bid and liquidity checks. |
| Realized-only results could hide open losses. | Show open-position marks after estimated exit costs, explicitly count unpriced positions, and label the equity/drawdown curves as realized only. |
| Daily UTC date labels could be shifted to the previous date in New York. | Preserve provider daily session-date labels and exclude current-day daily candles from context. |
| CPI's `US-Eastern` timezone alias was not recognized. | Parse the actual official feed alias and test DST conversion. Failed/expired calendars remain flagged. |
| Saving paper settings lacked an explicit submit-button type; periodic updates could overwrite a form being edited. | Add the submit type and preserve focus while editing the paper form. |
| Mobile grid tracks could expand to the width of tables and navigation. | Use shrinkable grid tracks, contained horizontal tables/navigation and wrapping controls; verified at 390px. |
| New installs could launch before the log directory existed. | Create the local data directory before starting the server. |

Existing EMA SMA seed, Wilder ATR, previous-20-bar volume baseline, closed-candle evaluation, next-bar replay fills, stop-first ambiguity rule and worse-open stop gaps were checked. Tests cover known arithmetic examples, incomplete/future candles, signal identities, sessions, risk limits and conservative outcomes.

## Bias and data limitations that code alone cannot remove

- **Survivorship:** the current watchlist is a selected universe. Historical S&P membership and delisted securities are not available. Replays disclose this; dated watchlist snapshots are retained going forward. This does not make historical replays survivorship-free.
- **Historical options:** no complete historical bid/ask chain archive is available. JARVIS rejects historical options P/L simulation; forward paper options use later observed asks/bids and configurable contract fees. Underlying outcomes are not options returns.
- **Historical context:** old point-in-time VIX, earnings calendars, benchmark snapshots and contemporaneous reports are not available for arbitrary imports. Historical replays exclude live context/event gates and state this prominently. Do not compare that replay directly with the entire live pipeline.
- **Execution:** quotes are observed, not guaranteed fills. Depth, queue position, borrow availability/charges, latency and some intrabar paths are not modeled. Stock-short results are hypothetical. A stop can lose more than its planned amount.
- **Portfolio:** a historical replay is one symbol and strategy. Separate runs must not be summed into a portfolio. Forward paper positions share cash, trade-count and loss guards.
- **Open losses:** realized drawdown excludes unrealized losses. Fresh liquidation marks are shown separately; missing quotes leave exposure open, not profitable or flat by assumption.
- **News and confidence:** headline themes and related coverage are not verified causation. Scores are fixed evidence heuristics, not calibrated win probabilities. Matched forward confidence needs 100 observations across 20 sessions; a daily improvement is not promised.
- **IV:** the forward IV series is a 7–21 DTE near-ATM call proxy, sampled after 15:45 Eastern only while the relevant chain is selected and fresh. It requires 252 prior observed sessions. It is not a standardized constant-maturity provider IV index.
- **Crypto derivatives:** public funding is displayed where supplied. Coinbase US open interest and liquidation data are unavailable through the connected feed. JARVIS does not substitute unrelated offshore instruments.
- **Calendars:** CPI and FOMC refresh from official sources. FOMC blocks the entire decision day because the schedule provides dates. Earnings timing is approximate and tentative dates are flagged. Empty/stale calendars never establish absence of event risk. Unscheduled events remain possible.

## 2026-10-14 quote freshness follow-up
